LudoNix — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026. App: LudoNix (package com.neonludo.arena). Contact: krishphy25@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

Your player names, settings, scores and the photo you choose for a player are stored only on your device. The photo is never uploaded. We have no accounts and no login.

Online rooms

If you use an online room, the name you typed, the room code and your game moves (dice results, goti moves, ready-made chat phrases and emoji) are sent through a relay server (Cloudflare Workers) to the other players in the same room. Nothing is stored after the room closes. Free-text chat is not possible.

Advertising

LudoNix shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, do not ask for your contacts, location, microphone or camera permissions (the photo picker is used only if you choose to add a player photo), and do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your choices

Clear the app's storage in Android Settings to remove all local data. For questions or deletion requests, email krishphy25@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
